\chapter*{ABSTRACT}

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent\textbf{Abstract:} Game balancing is one of the most critical and labor-intensive challenges in game development, particularly for Roguelike Deckbuilder games, where hundreds of interconnected parameters - card damages, enemy statistics, relic effects, and map distributions - must coexist to produce a fair and engaging player experience. Traditional manual tuning is slow, error-prone, and incapable of systematically exploring the vast parameter space.

This thesis presents a complete automated game-balancing framework for a Roguelike Deckbuilder prototype built in Python~3.12, composed of three tightly integrated layers. The Core Game Layer implements full game logic: turn-based combat, a procedurally generated 15-floor map, a deckbuilding system with over 30 cards and 20 relics, and multiple room types. A rule-based Deterministic Heuristic Agent (DHA) was developed first to validate playability and establish baseline metrics.

The central contribution is a suite of four Reinforcement Learning (RL) agents trained via Maskable Proximal Policy Optimization (MaskablePPO), each embodying a distinct play style - Aggressive, Defensive, Balanced, and Adaptive - shaped through custom reward functions and trained over 14~million timesteps on GPU hardware. Invalid Action Masking and Domain Randomization are employed to improve training stability and agent generalization across varied game configurations. All four agents achieve win rates of 24 - 34\%, a realistic benchmark given the game's inherent stochastic elements.

The Optimization Layer employs two evolutionary approaches: a Single-Objective Genetic Algorithm (GA) operating on $\sim$500 direct parameters, and NSGA-II with a hierarchical genome of 38 parameters that optimizes three objectives simultaneously - Balance, Engagement, and Coherence. After 30~generations with a population of 50, NSGA-II achieves a Balance Score of 0.960, Engagement Score of 0.900, and Coherence of 0.984, eliminates all trap cards, and raises the number of viable cards from 12 to 14 - all in 49~minutes, 33\% faster than the single-objective GA.

This work demonstrates that combining hierarchical genome encoding, multi-objective optimization, and learned RL agents produces a robust and extensible automatic balancing framework that significantly outperforms both manual tuning and flat single-objective evolutionary approaches.

\noindent\textit{\textbf{Keywords:}} \textit{Roguelike Deckbuilder, NSGA-II, Reinforcement Learning, Game Balance Optimization.}
